I study how to estimate credible counterfactuals from panel data, particularly when the available comparison units differ from the units we want to learn about.

Research interests

  • Synthetic control
  • Machine learning for panel data
  • Causal inference with limited overlap

About

My work combines latent factor models and machine learning to study policy effects with panel data. I’m also interested in applications to urban economics, including how parcel characteristics relate to property values.

Teaching

I develop applied econometrics labs on time series, forecasting and panel data, with an emphasis on model diagnostics and how to interpret estimates.
