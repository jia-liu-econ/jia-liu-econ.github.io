Research

My research focuses on causal inference with panel data and the reliability of counterfactual estimates when comparable observations are scarce.

Synthetic control

I study how synthetic control and interactive fixed effects use pre-treatment outcomes to estimate what would have happened to treated units in the absence of an intervention.

Machine learning for panel data

I work on combining interactive fixed effects with flexible machine learning models. The goal is to capture nonlinear relationships while accounting for unobserved factors that evolve over time.

Causal inference with limited overlap

I’m interested in how counterfactual estimates depend on the overlap between the data used for estimation and the units or periods for which we want a prediction. A related question is how to adapt forecasts when new observations fall outside the range represented in the training data.
