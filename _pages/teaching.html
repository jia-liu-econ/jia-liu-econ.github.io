Teaching

I develop applied econometrics labs that connect model assumptions, estimation and interpretation.

Time series and forecasting

Unit roots, cointegration and error correction models. The labs use diagnostics to relate model specification to the series’ long-run behaviour.

Vector autoregressions

Reduced-form and structural VARs, impulse responses and the role of identifying assumptions in interpreting shocks.

Panel data

Fixed and random effects, the Hausman test and Nickell bias in dynamic panels.
